In your photo
Described by Claude

In your photo, there is a round patch of red, raised skin on a hairy arm or leg. The edges look ring-shaped and uneven, with clearer skin toward the center.

Some of these can be signs of a fungal skin infection (tinea), but not all of them.

About

A fungal skin infection is caused by a fungus. It is also called ringworm, but no worm is involved. Fungus grows well in hot, damp places, so it is common in Malaysia. It can spread by touch or shared towels.

Common signs

A red or darker, ring-shaped or spreading patch with a scaly, raised edge that is often itchy. Common in hot, humid weather and in areas that stay damp, such as the groin, feet and skin folds.

What you can do

Keep the area clean and dry. Do not put steroid creams on it unless a doctor advises, because they can make fungal infections worse. See a doctor or pharmacist if it spreads or does not improve.
